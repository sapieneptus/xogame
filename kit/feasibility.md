# Feasibility tiers

Use this before agreeing to any feature. Be kind and clear when saying no.

## Green (fine for version 1)
- Single-player 2D games
- Tap, click, drag or any-key controls
- Simple scoring and progress (stars filling up), fixed levels, a handful of screens
- Sounds from the kit or generated by the kit's sound recipes, and calm music
- Voice lines recorded by the grown-up in the Voice Studio
- Shapes, characters and scenes drawn in code; the kit's mascot
- Matching, sorting, finding, counting, colouring, popping, catching and "make something happen" games
- Learning content delivered by pictures and voice (colours, shapes, letters, numbers, animals)

## Yellow (later versions, with a stated cost)
- Installing on the kids' Fire tablets as an app (APK) - one-time setup, may need an extra pair of hands
- Saving progress on the device
- Many levels or lots of hand-made art
- Using the kids' own drawings or photos (needs scanning/cropping)
- Speech synthesis (computer voices) - prefer recorded lines
- Physics-heavy play, or anything needing a lot of tuning to feel good
- More than one child playing at once on the same screen

## Red (not in version 1; offer the simple alternative)
- Online or multiplayer play -> taking turns on one device
- Accounts or login -> nothing to log into; maybe a picture to pick "who's playing" (yellow)
- In-app purchases or ads -> never, for these games
- Publishing to an app store or hosting online -> keep it on the family's own devices
- Speech recognition or listening through the microphone during play -> tap to answer instead
- Sharing things online, chat, anything that collects data about children -> never
