# Playtest

Write 3 to 5 numbered steps they can do on the game that's now open, each saying what they should see or hear. No technical words. Example:
1. Tap anywhere. A big shape should pop up with a little "pop" sound.
2. Tap nine more times. The stars at the top fill up, then confetti and a fanfare.
3. Hold Ctrl + Shift + Q for two seconds. The game closes.

If the kids are around, suggest one thing to watch for ("Does she understand what to tap without being told?").

Then ask: "Did everything work? If something felt wrong, tell me which step and what happened."
If something is wrong: reproduce it in a test if you can, fix it, re-run `test.sh`, and give a short checklist for just that part.
